Identity Resolving Key (IRK)

Apple Watches change their Bluetooth address every 15 minutes. The IRK allows WristKey to recognize your watch through rotating MAC addresses.

Option A: Automatic Import from Windows Registry Recommended

Windows stores pairing keys in HKLM\...\BTHPORT\Parameters\Keys when your watch is paired with Windows Bluetooth.
