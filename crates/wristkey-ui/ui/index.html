Identity Resolving Key (IRK)

Apple Watches change their Bluetooth address every 15 minutes. The IRK allows WristKey to recognize your watch through rotating MAC addresses.

ℹ️ La clave IRK es opcional: Si tu Apple Watch solo está vinculado a tu iPhone (lo normal), puedes ignorar este paso y pulsar directamente en "Next: Distance Calibration ➔" abajo para usar la detección por proximidad del reloj seleccionado.

Windows DPAPI Credential Vault

To seamlessly unlock Windows when your watch is in proximity, store your credentials protected with Windows Data Protection API (DPAPI).

Hardware-Grade OS Security: Credentials are encrypted with your personal Windows account key. No plaintext password is ever written to disk or sent over the network.
